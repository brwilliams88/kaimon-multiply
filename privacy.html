Privacy

Kaimon Multiply is a free times tables game for kids. Here is what it keeps, what it sends, and how to turn things off.

The short version

  • No ads. We never sell or share your data.
  • You don't need an account to play. Progress stays on your device.
  • Emails only go to grown-ups who sign up, and every email has a way to stop.
  • We count how the game is used, with a rough city but never emails, names of real people or exact locations. A grown-up can turn this off.

Progress on your device

Trainer names, answers, Kaimon caught and settings are saved in the browser on this device. Guest trainers (friends playing on your device) never leave it.

To erase it, delete the trainer in Grown-ups, or clear this site's data in the browser settings.

Family sync (optional)

If a grown-up turns on family sync, the family's trainers and their progress are stored with Supabase so every family device stays in step. Sign-in uses the grown-up's email and a one-time code.

Each family can only see its own trainers. Turning sync off keeps progress on the device. A grown-up can delete a trainer everywhere from Grown-ups.

Weekly reports (optional)

A grown-up can add up to 5 emails to get a short weekly report about one trainer. Each person gets one "say yes" email first, and reports only go to people who tap yes. Every report has a Stop these emails link.

The game sends the week's numbers and a small picture of the trainer to our Google Apps Script mailer, which builds the email and sends it from the Kaimon Gmail account. It keeps only who said yes, which weeks were sent and today's email count. It does not keep progress or pictures.

News emails

The grown-up who turns on family sync can get emails about what's new in Kaimon games: weekly for the first few weeks after launch, then about once a month. The box is checked when you sign up, and you can uncheck it. You can turn news off any time in Grown-ups, or tap Unsubscribe in any news email, which deletes your email from the list.

Emails that a grown-up types in for weekly reports are never added to the news list.

Ratings and feedback

When you rate the game or send feedback, we keep what you send: the stars, your note, a name if you give one, a reply email if a grown-up adds one, the app version and a random device number. It goes only to the maker of the game, by email and in a private list. Please don't put anything private in a note.

Usage counts

To see if the game is fun and working, it sends simple counts to a private dashboard:

  • a random number made for this device (not linked to your email or family sync)
  • trainer names
  • the kind of device and browser, and whether the game is installed
  • app opens, rounds, battles, Kaimon caught and minutes played
  • where the game was first opened from: a shared link like "WhatsApp", or just the name of the site that sent you, like linkedin.com (never the full web address)
  • a rough place: city, region and country
  • the device's time zone, and the local day and hour of play (so we know when kids like to play)

For the rough place, once a day the device asks a free lookup service (GeoJS, or ipapi if that is busy) where its internet address is. We keep only the city, region and country. The internet address itself is never sent to us or stored.

No emails, real names or photos are collected this way. A grown-up can turn usage counts off in Grown-ups. Turning them off also forgets the device number and the place.

No ads, no tracking

There are no ads, no tracking cookies and no third-party trackers. The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, sync and counts use Supabase, and emails use Google. Like any website, these services see basic connection details such as IP addresses to deliver the game. We don't store them.

Questions or deleting data

Use Send feedback in the game's Menu and add your email so we can reply, or reply to any email from Kaimon Multiply. We can delete your family's synced data, your feedback or your email from any list.
